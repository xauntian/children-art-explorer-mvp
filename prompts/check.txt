clearly shows the requested visual style. Unchanged rendering or a mere hue change

The legacy field content_preserved means theme association, not exact object preservation.
Return true when the original theme or some recognisable visual cues still connect the images.

positions or composition, and repainting all materials and the background are allowed.
Do not reject merely for those changes. Return false when the candidate is wholly unrelated,
and null when the evidence is insufficient to decide the connection. Do not infer a connection
merely because the candidate was requested or because both images are paintings.
